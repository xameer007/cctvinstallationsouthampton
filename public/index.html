How much does CCTV installation cost in Southampton?+
A 2-camera home system starts from £349 fully installed. A 4 to 6 camera commercial system typically costs £699 to £1,200 depending on camera type, cabling, and NVR spec. We provide fixed-price quotes after a free survey with no hidden charges.
Do I need planning permission for outdoor CCTV in Southampton?+
In most cases, no. CCTV cameras mounted over 2.5 metres high and projecting less than 1 metre from an external wall do not require planning permission in Southampton. However, listed buildings and conservation areas may need consent — our surveyor checks this free of charge during your site visit.
Is CCTV installation in Southampton GDPR and ICO compliant?+
Yes. All our installations follow the ICO Code of Practice on CCTV and meet UK GDPR requirements. We advise on signage, data retention periods (typically 30 to 90 days), and whether your system requires ICO registration. We guide you through every requirement as part of the installation.
Can I view my CCTV cameras remotely on a smartphone?+
Yes — all installations include full remote access configuration. We set up iOS and Android apps allowing live footage viewing, recording review, and motion-alert notifications from anywhere in the world.
What is the difference between IP CCTV and analogue systems?+
IP (Internet Protocol) cameras transmit digital footage over a network and offer up to 4K resolution, smart analytics (ANPR, facial detection), and easy scalability. Analogue systems use coaxial cable to a DVR and are cost-effective for smaller properties. We recommend the best option for your needs during the free survey.
Do you offer emergency CCTV installation in Southampton?+
Yes. Following a break-in or urgent security situation, we offer same-day and next-day emergency installations across Southampton and Hampshire. Call 023 8000 0000 and we will prioritise your booking for the fastest available appointment.
Do you provide CCTV maintenance contracts in Southampton?+
Yes. Annual maintenance contracts cover scheduled health checks, lens cleaning, firmware updates, DVR/NVR testing, and priority fault response with a guaranteed 4-hour callback SLA for all commercial clients across Hampshire.
